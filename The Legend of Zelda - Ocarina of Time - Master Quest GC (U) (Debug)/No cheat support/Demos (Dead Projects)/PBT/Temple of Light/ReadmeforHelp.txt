Now to play this demo you are gonna need to do a couple of things.

First off is installing the patch.

Open up the Program called ppf-Omatic

Look for your ZELDAOOTMA.Z64 or whatever you named your debug rom

(p.S sadly I cannott disubute the rom on my own, so you will have to find it on your own :( )

Though you would have to change the option to "All files" instead of CD images


After that find the .ppf (the patch)
It should be called
"TempleofLightDemo1.ppf"
Then you would use the program to patch the rom.
.
Im not sure if it matters.

But I reccomend using Pj64 1.6 or 1.7.

I have no history with Nemu or any other N64 emulator. So I have no idea how it works.

Advance Settings must be turned on for everything in the emulator.
For the Debug Rom the games memnory size must be 8mb

Load up the savestate (The savestate is named "Loadthissave") and Enjoy!
