Star Revenge 3.5 v1.3
	Vacation of Cursed Dreams

This Super Mario 64 Hack is a remake/expansion of Star Revenge 3: Mario on An Saoire.
This hack merely uses the same story and level themes as SR3. Levels, Music, Course 9 - 15 and more are
all new stuff added with this remake.
The hack itself has 132 stars. It starts off easy, but if you go for 100% there will be hard stuff
along the way in the later courses. Any% for this is 50 stars.
A main thing for this hack is, that the new courses 9 to 15 are all 2.5D courses. This is to fully
realize the original idea of SR3 being based off M&L Dream Team.
All stars are hinted at in the game, so read some of the text boxes and don't complain you
can't find stuff... even tho this isn't as cryptic and full of secrets as SR2: To The Moon was.
