Star Revenge 8
   - Scepter of Hope v1.3

Hack by BroDute
Original game by Nintendo

More credits on the first sign ingame.

This hack has 121 stars, all levels replaced and stuff. This here is one of those
open world hacks like SR3 or 4 aka there is no real overworld, so you go from level
to level. The starting area has 2 shortcuts to later levels if you got the keys from bosses.

This hack is pretty Touhou based, so you will hear a lot of Touhou music, if you have a
problem with that, then get a cheat code that disables music and play our own while playing.

The map is there to help you find the ways to levels quicker, if you are looking for
something. If there is a thing on a arrow, that means you need that thing to use the warp.
If a symbol is next to a name, it means you get that item there!

Story whise: You are Mario, earth got destroyed and you try to get your hands on the
Scepter of Faith to bring earth back to what it once was by collecting stars and power ups
to reach his goal.

Difficulty: Normal standert for my hacks!
